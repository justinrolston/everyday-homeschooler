Bunny Themed Book Basket

Mar 19
Written by Sarah Rolston

Spring has finally sprung here in the midwest! Temperatures have warmed up and we actually get to see the sun from time to time!

That means it is time for me to retire our Snow Themed Book Basket and start something new.

Easter is just a few short weeks away - Glory Hallelujah - but I would really like for this book basket to last longer than just a few days. I need something that will last a good month or so into the spring.

So I’ve decided to create a bunny themed book basket. Rabbits, bunnies, hares… honestly any type of cute little fur ball will do! Something seasonal, without being completely tied to the Easter holiday!
