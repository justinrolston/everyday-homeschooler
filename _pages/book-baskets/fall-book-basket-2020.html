Fall Book Basket

Oct 2
Written by Sarah Rolston

Autumn Picture Books 2020

There is nothing quite like a basket full of books!

I am convinced that keeping picture books at arm’s length is the key to encouraging my children’s’ love of reading.

I love to tuck basketfuls of picture books around our house for my kids to dig into.

A stack of colorful board books will tempt even the most active toddler to curl up on the couch and read.

One of my favorite book baskets is the one we keep next to fireplace in our living room. Every month or so I update it with seasonal books from the library or second hand store.
