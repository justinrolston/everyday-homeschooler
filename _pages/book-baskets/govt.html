Written by Sarah Rolston

Tired of the political ads, yard signs, and heated conversations at the dinner table?

It’s election season!

Did I just hear you groan?

Um yeah, me too!

So I’ve determined to be positive and make this season fun - for myself and for my kids.

This is the perfect time to make a Government themed book basket! A basket full of picture books dedicated to our government system, founding fathers, & presidents.

The basket I am putting together this year is going to pair with Gather ‘Round Homeschool’s U.S. Government Mini Unit.
