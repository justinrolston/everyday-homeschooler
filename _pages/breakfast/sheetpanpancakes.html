Sheet Pan Pancakes

Feb 11
Written by Sarah Rolston

Don’t waste time flipping dozens of pancakes. Cook them all at once instead!

Make several flavors at once: blueberry, pecan, chocolate chip - all on the same sheet pan.

Great to feed a crowd… or just a bunch of hungry kids!

Print Recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 1/2 cups flour

  • 1 Tbsp baking powder

  • 1 t baking soda

  • 1/2 tsp salt

  • 1 tsp cinnamon

  • 1/3 cup sugar

  • 2 cups whole milk

  • 2 eggs

  • 2 tsp vanilla

  • 1 stick butter (melted and cooled)

  • 1/2 cup sourdough discard (optional)

  • various toppings of choice (optional): blueberries, chocolate chips, pecans, sprinkles, etc.

  • butter and warm maple syrup to serve

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees.

  2. Line a sheet pan with parchment paper. Lightly coat with cooking oil to grease.

  3. Stir dry ingredients together in a bowl: flour, baking powder, soda, salt, cinnamon, and sugar.

  4. Add milk, eggs, and vanilla. Gently stir until combined.

  5. Stir in melted butter and sourdough discard if desired.

  6. Pour batter onto parchment paper. Spread with a spatula until even.

  7. Sprinkle various toppings of choice over batter in different sections - or leave plain.

  8. Bake for 15-18 minutes.

  9. Optional: Broil for 1-2 minutes, keeping a constant eye on the pancake, to lightly brown the top of your pancakes.

  10. Remove from oven. Use a pizza cutter to slice into large squares.

  11. Serve with warm butter and syrup.

Notes

I often just use all-purpose flour for this recipe, but I also enjoy using a combiniation of half whole wheat flour and half all-purpose. This will make your pancake a little heavier, but the multigrain experience is delicious.

This recipe is great to use up your sourdough discard. It will give your pancakes a little extra lift and tang!
